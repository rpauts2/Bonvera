# BONVERA image review assets

This folder contains draft compositions for internal visual review only. Nothing here replaces live offer images or is deployed to the Mini App.

## T-Bank Black sample

- `official/tbank-black-official-product.png` is a direct product image retrieved from T-Bank's official Black debit-card page: https://www.tbank.ru/cards/debit-cards/tinkoff-black/
- Source image URL: https://cdn.tbank.ru/static/pfa-multimedia/images/9b90398a-ef0a-4148-89a7-3b85ab056150.png
- `compositions/tbank-black-bonvera-preview.svg` adds a BONVERA dark-violet / gold visual stage around the source image.
- The product image is embedded byte-for-byte in the SVG. It is not redrawn, recolored, warped, cropped or retouched.
- The source and composition are NOT approved for production reuse until the bank/affiliate partner confirms commercial display rights.

## Production gate

Do not copy review assets into `assets/offers/` until exact product matching, current issuance status, and commercial image-use rights are verified. If rights are not confirmed, request a partner media kit or written approval.
